# Dev friction analysis (2026-11-17)

## How it was run

- **Finders:** 10 agents, one per lens: content touchpoints, UI touchpoints, engine candidates, half-finished media, half-finished visual and platform work, process overhead, tooling sprawl, tests and gates, roadmap against effort, and hotspots and architecture. Each one read git history, tickets and code, and opened the private content copy read-only.
- **Skeptics:** 10 more agents, each re-measuring one finder's claims. Where a number was off, this report uses the skeptic's corrected number.
- **Synthesizer:** 1 agent merged duplicates and dropped refuted claims.
- **Scope:** this report complements the PR retro of the same day (`docs/research/retro-2026-11-17-prs-616-735.md`). Anything that retro already filed is listed only when this pass found a deeper cause or a bigger scope, and then the table names the ticket it extends.
- **Verdicts:** *Confirmed* means the skeptic reproduced the numbers. *Weakened* means the finding is real but smaller than first claimed, and the corrected numbers are shown. *Covered* means an open ticket already does it.

## Headline

- **Ch2-5 are playable now, but the Act 1 playtest will show them half-finished, and its ticket doesn't say so.**
  - 20 speakers who appear on screen have no face.
  - Chapters 2-8 have 0 voice clips.
  - 3 enemy classes draw a stand-in figure: about 22% of placed units.
  - Promotion can't be reached because no shop or battle gives a seal.
  - Supports and camp have empty data tables.
  - The art tickets for those faces (0861, 0862, 0866) are not on the playtest's (1064) blocker list. Nothing in the repo measures these gaps.
- **One unmade owner action blocks 13 tickets and the playtest.** Hiring the writer (0766) is ready to do. 13 tickets wait on it, and the Act 1 playtest waits on it indirectly through 1073, 1083 and 1084. It isn't in any decision queue.
- **The game keeps working on copies of features the engine already ships.**
  - The engine's playtest tools have been done for 3+ weeks. Since then the game's own copy took 20 more PRs (+977 lines), and 0173 would build its CI a second time.
  - The engine's Playdate backend has been ready since 11-10, yet the game keeps about 7,250 lines of its own Playdate platform code and changed it in 27 commits.
- **Some hand-kept copies charge every PR:**
  - The test language pack is an all-capitals copy of every English line, hand-edited in 40 of 41 text PRs and enforced by a test.
  - Whole-screen snapshots re-save about 18,300 lines a month. One hint restyle touched 121 snapshot files.
  - Every chapter battle needs a hand-chained test file plus about 8 small edits in other files.
- **Process work grows faster than it is cleared.**
  - 5 retros and audits filed 89 tickets in 26 days, and 13 are done.
  - The rules every session must read went from about 400 to 851 lines in seven weeks.
  - 6 open tickets (891 lines) exist only to police the PR closing lines, which flagged a 3× overrun in 3 of 300 PRs.
- **Every screen change is paid twice.**
  - 60 of 122 recent UI commits also edited the one-bit Playdate copy.
  - 12 screens still have no one-bit look.
  - 14 Playdate-only tickets sit on the Act 1 gate.
