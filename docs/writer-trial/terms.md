# The packet and your entry, in plain words

This page is a summary. **The two agreements you signed with Ibasho Corp.
before getting this packet, the confidentiality agreement and the Writer
Trial and AI Voice Acknowledgment, are what count**; where this page
differs, they win. "I" on this page is Nick, writing for Ibasho Corp.

## Who can apply

You must be 18 or older. Applicants from almost any country are welcome.
Ibasho Corp. is a US company, so it can't take work from or pay anyone
who lives in a country or region under a US embargo, or who is on a US
sanctions list. Today (November 2026) the embargoed places are:

- Cuba
- Iran
- North Korea
- the Crimea region of Ukraine, and the areas of Ukraine called the
  Donetsk and Luhansk "People's Republics"

If you aren't sure whether that includes you, ask me before you start.

If the US adds the country or region where you live to that list later,
I have to stop paying you from that day. If your work for the game isn't
finished by then, our agreement ends and I'll hire another writer to
finish it.

## The packet

- It's confidential. Please don't share it, post it or show it to anyone,
  in whole or in part: the scripts, the story pages, and the links and
  passwords of the demo and the script preview.
- It's marked as yours, so a copy that turns up somewhere can be traced
  back to the packet it came from.
- When the trial is over and you aren't the writer picked, please delete
  it.

## Your entry

- **Yours stays yours.** I read your entry and compare it with the
  others, and that's all I do with it, unless I hire you and we agree in
  writing to use it.
- I won't publish it, put it in the game or pass it to anyone outside
  the project.
- **Your ideas too.** If your note suggests a change to the story and I
  want to use it, whether or not I hire you, I'll ask you first, and
  we'll agree in writing on credit and pay before it goes in. If I had
  already planned the same change, or another applicant suggested it
  too, I'll tell you so plainly.

## The voices

The game's spoken lines are made with AI voice synthesis. If I hire you,
the lines you write for the game will be given to a voice model to make
them spoken. Your trial entry won't be, unless I hire you and we agree to
use it. I won't record, clone or synthesize your own voice, and I won't
use your writing to train an AI model. If you have any concern about
this, ethical or otherwise, tell me right away.

## The job

The trial is unpaid. What the writer I pick is offered (a share of the
game's revenue after launch) will be in a written agreement before any
work beyond the trial. If either of us needs to stop partway through,
either side can end it with two weeks' notice. The agreement says what
happens to work already done.
