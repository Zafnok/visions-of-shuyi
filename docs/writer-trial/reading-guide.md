# The writer trial: the entries

Each entry is a column, named by a letter in a random order, so you read
them without names. The first column is the current AI draft, to compare
against. Which entry is whose is in compare-key.txt beside this page: open
it only after you've picked.

## What to look at

Your own words on what was wrong with the drafts:

> the manner of speaking is robotic and awful

> Have more warmth without cringey jokes every 2 lines. Just talk more human

- Does it sound like people talking, not robotic?
- Does each character sound like their sheet's voice card, and different from the others? (Fine if the writer changed a voice on purpose and said why in the note.)
- Is there warmth, without a joke every few lines?
- Does the lead stay within the lead's rules: a voice the player speaks through, whose replies are tones (earnest, wry, blunt), not a personality of their own?
- Would you want the rest of the game written like this?

The problem count is how many things the game's checks found in the whole entry. A few are format slips anyone can fix; judge the writing. Read each entry's note too (it came with the entry, in its folder).
