## What the chapter must do (the outline, `../outline.md`)

- **Goal:** find someone who can stop the war. The Brennish envoy in
  Saltmere, Ragna Holt, needs proof that the "Brennish raids" are
  staged, and Joss is a Hound willing to testify.
- **Conflict:** the Saltmere League is neutral and for sale. A magistrate
  sells the envoy's safe house to the Hounds. Aske's secret comes out:
  the Brennish smuggler Aske sold march news to also sells to Ragna's
  people, which is how Ragna knows her name. The raiders who bought it
  are real, hungry hill bands; the Hounds' false flags hide behind them.
- **Turn:** the envoy gets Joss's testimony, but the League expels
  everyone involved. Ragna: both armies are massing at the Ashfields.
  "If you want to stop this war, you stop it there, or it's the Ashfields
  again."
- **Main-story scene:** the night before the battle, Hollis tells the
  lead everything: the purse, the list, the stamped pass, and why he
  burned them.
- **Arcs advanced:** Aske (her secret), Hollis (confession), the lead and
  Hollis (mending); from the sheets, Joss (three tellings, and the third
  time he says he sewed the badges himself: the first time aloud),
  Ragna (she says "war" for the first time), Hedda (lent, sour and
  polite: "I'm not yours, outlaw. I'm lent.").
- **Leave for later:** that the proof is believed and doesn't matter
  (Chapter 6); Wren alive (Chapter 6; Rue says nothing of it here);
  Oriel; the Door; what Hollis makes of the sea when he dies on these
  docks (Chapter 8). Nobody in this chapter says the word "Door" or
  learns anything of the king.

## Where Aske's secret comes out

The outline puts it in the chapter's conflict, not its turn, and leaves
the moment open. This sheet puts it **after the hold, on the quay**, not
in the envoy's house: the three scenes before the first move (the house,
the steps, the attack) are capped at 28 boxes between them, and the
house already has two new faces and Joss's first telling to carry. So in
the house Ragna only looks at her once ("the archer sits where I can see
her; we'll talk, later"), which Aske has dreaded since the road south,
and the player carries that through the night; on the quay, when she is
done with the party, Ragna says the name once, in front of everyone, and
pays what she owes a source: the name, and the boat (the hook for
*Letters Home*). The lead answers it there (choice 3). A rewrite may move
it back into the house if the cap allows.

## The sea

Hollis has never seen the sea (Chapter 1: the one thing the boy wrote
home about from the war that he believed). Saltmere is a port, and
Chapter 8 kills him on its docks. This sheet spends the sight once, on
the Salt Road's ridge after the guard skirmish, plainly ("it's a lot of
water"), and lets the confession happen within sound of it; Dace, in the
cut at the end, knows the old man has seen it now, and guesses his
words. Nobody makes a speech about it.

## Scenes

### 1. The road south (`ch05_intro`)

- **Where:** first light, a night's walking out of Veyne Hall, the
  valley behind them and the road bending south toward the salt country;
  wet grass, breath showing. Plays when the chapter opens (the world map
  appears). Music: `talk_calm`.
- **Who:** the lead, Tamsin, Hollis, Joss, Aske, Maud, Rue, Gil (each in
  an `@if`).
- **What happens:** Hollis is at the back, for the first time anyone can
  remember; Tamsin asks why, loud, and gets his knee. Joss has an idea
  and is scared it's stupid: somebody who can stop it has to be told by
  somebody who was there, and he was there; he'd say it to anyone who'd
  write it down. Aske, flat, names who would write it down: the woman
  who signed the peace, whom her whole hold curses by name, who sits in
  Saltmere writing letters; she does not want to go to Saltmere and gives
  a wrong reason (the League sells to anyone). Tamsin: Saltmere it is,
  then, two days over the downs, and she knows the price of a bed there.
  Maud has the register under her robe against the wet and asks whether
  the sea is cold (she has never seen it either). Rue eats, walking, and
  puts bread in the lead's hand. Gil (if there) has taken the ditch side
  of the road without a word about it. Hollis, from the back: the lead's
  shoulder, once ("it's dropped since the hall"), and nothing else.
- **Purpose:** the goal and why this road; Joss not okay; Aske not
  wanting to go; the crack between Hollis and the lead shown by where he
  walks.
- **Changes:** *plan* (Saltmere, the envoy, Joss's word), *relationship*
  (Hollis at the back).

### 2. The Salt Road (`ch05_salt_road_intro`, `ch05_salt_road_victory`)

- **Where:** the road over the downs, salt carts turned back on the
  verge, a Hound post at the march stone where Ardeval ends and the
  League's country begins. Guards the path to Saltmere. Music: the
  `skirmish` pool (the battle file's); the ridge after it, `talk_calm`.
- **Who:** Joss, Tamsin, Aske, Hollis, Maud (each in an `@if`).
- **Before (3–6):** the carters have been turned back, salt and all;
  Joss knows the post's shape (two at the stone, the rest in the hut) and
  knows nobody out here; Tamsin: past that stone they're the League's
  problem and the Hounds know it. Aske stands very still, looking south
  over the ridge, and says nothing. Hollis: Veyne in the middle; nobody
  runs for the stone.
- **After (2–4):** over the ridge the road drops and **the sea is there**.
  Hollis stops walking and says one plain thing about it, and the lead
  looks at his face, not the sea; behind them nobody speaks, not even
  Tamsin. Maud: they said big at the school, and she thought, a lake.
- **Changes:** *plan* (the path to Saltmere is open), *relationship*
  (the lead sees Hollis see the sea).

### 3. Smugglers' Cove (`ch05_cove_intro`, `ch05_cove_victory`)

- **Where:** a cove under the cliffs south of the road, boats pulled up,
  casks under tarred cloth, men with knives who are nobody's soldiers.
  Optional, with loot (1071's). Music: the `skirmish` pool.
- **Who:** Tamsin, Rue, Aske, Hollis, Gil (each in an `@if`).
- **Before (3–6):** Tamsin: a year's pay in casks and nobody's crest on

  hill-holds; Aske knows its marks).

### 4. The envoy's house (`ch05_envoy`)

- **Where:** a tall narrow house on the harbor front, shutters half
  closed against the southern heat, a writing case open on the table,
  ink on a cuff. Hedda fills the doorway. The first of the battle's
  intro scenes (0851 wires it; if 1007's chapter format lets a scene play
  when the story-battle node is first entered, play it there). Music:
  `talk_calm`.
- **Who:** the lead, Ragna, Hedda; Joss, Maud, Aske (each in an `@if`;
  Ragna and Hedda need none yet).
- **What happens:** Hedda in the door with the axe: which of you is the
  outlaw; wipe your feet, she washed that floor. Ragna, from the table,
  knows already: two weeks from Kell's Ford to her door by her count, the
  exile, the Hound boy who stood in a river, three letters about them;
  which of you is Pellam. **Joss testifies**: Kell's Ford, the
  lieutenant, the colors, the herald's paper sealed a week early, in the
  drill voice; it holds. Ragna: tomorrow again, and the day after, and
  then it's in three hands and holds in front of anyone; eat, you've gone
  gray. Maud looks at Ragna's eyes and says she hasn't slept; Hedda lies
  for her ("she sleeps fine"); Ragna: a month, thereabouts; sit, you're

- **Purpose:** the envoy and what she needs; the testimony begun; two

- **Changes:** *information* (Ragna has the first telling), *plan*

### 5. The sea steps (`ch05_eve`)

- **Where:** the harbor stairs under the house, night, the tide loud on
  the stones, ships' lamps on the water. Hollis sitting with his knee out
  and his hands empty. Plays before the battle, after the house, before
  the attack. Music: `scene_confide` (Nick, 0071); `scene_sad` stands in
  until 0851 imports it.
- **Who:** the lead and Hollis, nobody else. Without Hollis: the lead
  alone on the steps, and Rue (if alive) with bread, who sits and says
  she isn't asking.
- **What happens:** he starts with the sea: it's loud, he thought it
  would be quiet, the way the boy wrote it; sit down, you're in the
  light. Then **the whole of it, said plainly**: the winter after the
  exile, going back for the lead's things, the boy's old room; a purse
  of the mill man's silver in the chest, a list of names in the boy's
  hand with some crossed out, a blank road pass with the lead's father's
  wax on it; he sat with them all night in the guardroom and burned them
  before the house woke, and told the lead there was nothing in the room
  worth keeping. **Why:** the lead had given the boy everything, and
  Hollis wasn't going to be the one to say what it bought; he thought
  the lead couldn't carry it, which was wrong: it was him that couldn't.
  **The big feeling, once:** the lead was his from the night they walked
  out of that gate, more than the boy ever let himself be, and he let the
  lead walk four years beside a lie because he's a coward about them.
  "That's all of it." **Choice 1:** the lead's reply (earnest: you
  should have told me, I'd have carried it with you / blunt: four years,
  and a fire / an act: move over); each way he answers in one or two
  boxes. Then an order about boots and the watch, and "kid", and the
  door tomorrow: the lead stands in it, he's behind. He looks at the
  water, for a long time.
- **Purpose:** the outline's main-story scene and Hollis's arc beat; the
  mending (he says kid; the lead's shoulder is his again before the scene
  ends).
- **Changes:** *information* (the lead knows everything Hollis knows),
  *relationship* (the lead and Hollis: told, and mended enough).

### 6. The attack (`ch05_prebattle`)

- **Where:** the house, past midnight; a hand over a lamp; boots on the
  quay and a tile sliding off the roof next door. Ragna already dressed.
  Music: `dungeon_tense` (the battle's own cue, `battle_night_siege`, is
  the battle file's).
- **Who:** the lead, Ragna, Hedda; Tamsin, Joss, Maud, Hollis (each in
  an `@if`).
- **What happens:** Hedda, slow and low: easy, everybody; boots, a lot
  of them, on the quay, and that was a tile; Ragna behind the table, now,
  please. Ragna, crisp, counting: shutters, the back door, Pellam stays
  up here with her; the magistrate asked which room he slept in and she
  told him; **she thought a magistrate was a magistrate** (she is the one
  who got them sold, and says so flat). Tamsin, hot: "You told him?",
  then the roof hatch, black on three roofs. Joss, scared, does the
  drill under his breath (they come down the top of the stairs when the
  street door's held), can't count the roof, and asks to be put in a
  door anyway. Maud can't get the shutter bar across and asks for
  someone taller. Hollis: "Kid. The street door. I'm behind you."
  **Ragna sends Hedda from outside her own room down to the street
  door**: go down with them and hold it; she'll be behind this table with
  a knife, as she always is. Hedda refuses once ("I stand outside your
  room"), in the same breath gives in, and to the lead: down, then;
  outlaw; stand next to me. Nobody says how long until light: the
  objective does.
- **Purpose:** why this fight (the house is sold, by Ragna's own
  mistake; it's a hold till light); Hedda at the lead's side by Ragna's
  order; the whole house scared in its own way, and nobody the same calm
  commander.
- **Changes:** *stakes* (sold), *plan* (hold the door till light), *the
  army* (Hedda fights with them).

### 8. The morning (`ch05_victory`)

- **Where:** the house at first light, the shutters open, washing on a
  line across the way as if nothing. Music: `scene_sad`.
- **Who:** the lead, Ragna, the magistrate; Rue, Joss, Hedda (each in
  an `@if`).
- **What happens:** League halberds on the stair, and **the magistrate**

  envoy and every party to the night's disturbance outside the walls by
  the evening tide. Ragna: he dined at this table and asked which room
  the boy slept in. He has nothing but "the League is neutral, madam;
  I'm sorry; the evening tide", and goes. Rue: he didn't even come in;

  stops, and his hands go to his tin, which isn't there; then he says

  he was told and believed for one night; he knew by the second and
  finished them anyway; he was proud of the stitching. Ragna writes it
  and thanks him, once, briefly: that was the one she needed. He asks the
  lead to say something, anything. **Choice 2:** the lead to Joss
  (earnest: that took more than the river did / blunt: now it's said;
  sit down / an act: hand him his needle tin, which was under the

  never saw a badge sewn straight. Without Joss: Ragna takes the party's

- **Purpose:** the outline's turn, first half (the testimony got, the

- **Changes:** *information* (the proof exists, in Ragna's hand; the

### 9. The quay (`ch05_quay`)

  Noble. Say it." **Choice 3:** the lead to Aske (earnest: four years she

## Continuity notes

- The salt country is two days over the downs south of the heartland;
  the Salt Road crosses a march stone where Ardeval ends and the League's
  country begins. Saltmere is a harbor city on the sea; the envoy's house
  stands on the harbor front with stairs down to the water; the docks
  are on the water side and the roofs on the land side. The cove is
  under the cliffs south of the road. The Brennish ship that takes the
  party east in Chapter 8 is not this one; Ragna leaves in a ship's boat
  to a Brennish ship in the roads.
- The smuggler Aske sold to is never named and never seen. Ragna bought
  from him too; his list named Aske. The hill bands who bought her news
  are real raiders, hungry; what they raided with it is not said (the
  Hounds' raid at Kell's Ford was not theirs: Chapter 2).
- Ragna says "this trouble" until the quay, where she says "war" (in
  the outline's line, "stop this war").
- Joss's three tellings: the first (the house) holds; the second and
  third are the morning after: the second is only narration (he gets to
  the bag and stops), and in the third he says he sewed them. Ragna
  writes it all down. In the house she said "tomorrow, and the day
  after"; the expulsion made the day after the same morning.
- Hollis's confession names: the boy's old room at the hall, the winter
  after the exile; a purse of the mill man's silver; a list of names in
  the boy's hand, some crossed out; a blank road pass with the lead's
  father's wax; the guardroom fire before the house was up. He does not
  say "Dace". He says "kid" again before the scene ends, and "the way
  the boy wrote it" of the sea (Chapter 1: the boy wrote home about the
  sea from the war).
- Hollis sees the sea on the Salt Road ridge and says "It's a lot of
  water"; Dace guesses those words in the cut. Maud has never seen it
  either. Nobody makes a speech.
- Hedda calls the lead "outlaw" and Aske "hill girl" (not yet in this
  chapter's lines: her lines to Aske are 1073's); "little one" to anyone
  younger who is hurt (Joss, in the morning); "Easy" when she is scared.
  She knew Aske's name from Ragna's papers before the party came (her
  sheet); nothing in this chapter has her say so. Her carved boat is for
  Ragna's son, who is eight and has never been in a boat.
- Nobody says how many Hounds, how many turns, or the price on the
  lead: the map and the objective carry it. "The boy" is Hollis's word
  for Dace and nobody else's; Ragna says "Pellam" and "my boy" (her
  son).
- The Hound officer is nameless (a speaker, like the herald): a sergeant
  of Hendry's old company who knew Joss from his first week. Hendry's
  pay to his mother is Chapter 2's (Dace sent it); the officer knows.
- The magistrate is nameless (a speaker): he dined at the house the day
  before the attack and asked which room Joss slept in.
- Dace's cut: he is ordered to the Ashfields; nothing is said of Ossary
  or the old man at the gate.
- The lead's name: `{lead}`; "Veyne" and every other name is a token.
  Pronoun tokens only. No gendered words.
- The plain build has no portrait for anyone but the lead, so the script
  uses only the five expressions every character has.
