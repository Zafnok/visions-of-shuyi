# Lieutenant Silas Hendry

Id: `hound_lieutenant` · [Name registry](../names.md)

A short boss sheet (0743): want, voice, three lines.

## Role

- **The Chapter 2 boss** at Kell's Ford: the Hound lieutenant who leads
  the raid in Brennish colors. He dies there.
- **Class:** an axe user carrying the **Houndmaster's Axe**, and uses its
  art, Pack Hunt (0018, `combat-arts.md`, *Special weapons*). Class,
  level and stats: 0848.
- **Age:** 27.

## Who he is

A Thornmarch ford boy whose village was burned by real Brennish raiders
in the war's first year, when he was fourteen. He enlisted at fifteen,
served under Dace on the northern passes in the war's last year, and
was one of the first men Dace pulled into the Hounds. Dace gave him the
axe the day he made him lieutenant. He is a good officer: he knows his men's names and their mothers' names, he
shares his rations, and he picked Joss for the company himself and
called him "Needle".

- **Want:** the war, now, while Ardeval can still win it, and the
  captain's good opinion. He believes the war is coming anyway and that
  Brennmark should pay for the villages it burned in the last one.
- **His grievance, which a player could share:** the Ash Peace let the
  men who burned his village go home and never answer for it.
- **What he won't look at:** he is doing to Kell's Ford what was done to
  his own village, in the same colors. He knows. He calls it the same
  fire going back where it came from.
- **With Joss** (who shares his history, so a 2–4 box exchange): the
  boy he picked, who sewed the colors and then wouldn't use them.

## Voice

- Easy, friendly, a good officer's voice: first names, a joke for his
  men, plain orders. He is likable, which is the horror of him.
- Cornered, the friendliness stays and the patience goes.
- **Won't say:** "Brennish" about what his men are doing. "The job."
  "The village."
- **Punctuation:** plain periods and easy questions; one `!` when Joss
  turns.

**Sample lines** (never copied into a script)

- *Engage (anyone):* "Put it down and walk off. I've no orders about you,
  and I'd like to keep it that way."
- *With Joss:* "Needle. Come here. Come and stand by me, and we'll say
  you got lost in the smoke."
- *Defeat:* "My pay. Tell the captain to send my pay to my mother. ...He
  knows where."

## Planted

- **In his hands:** the Houndmaster's Axe, Dace's gift. It is the
  player's when the battle is won.

## Look

His combat picture is Stupid Creations' TTMini Bandit Brigand (ticket
0076, `look-and-feel.md`, *Act 1's new cast*), and this look was
rewritten to fit it. He speaks in Chapter 2's scenes, so he has a
generator face built to match.

- Stocky, in leather and mail with a red cloak, the Brennish badges
  Joss sewed on for the disguise, a round shield, and the axe. Shaggy red hair, a
  full red beard, an easy smile. Expressions: `neutral`, `happy`, `angry`,
  `sad`.
