# Rue

Id: `heretic` · [Name registry](../names.md)

## Role and class

- **Role:** playable companion from Chapter 1. She carries the truth about
  Dace and about Wren.
- **Class:** **Mage** (fire; swords in tiers 1–2).
- **Talent:** Mag.
- **Age:** 19.

## At a glance

A gifted girl from a Thornmarch charcoal-burners' family, taken twice:

- at 15 she was in Vosse's cart on the night at the mill, and escaped in the
  chaos after Vosse died;
- a year later she was caught by Vigil hunters, sworn to fire and made a
  **Candle**.

At 16 she burned at the Ashfields with the other Candles and deserted in the
smoke. There she swore a fire vow of her own, which makes her a heretic. Her
hands are scarred from that day. This autumn she took shelter with Keeper
Maud at Harrowby as her "cook" (she can't cook).

## Want, need, flaw, secret

- **Want:** to find the other children (the Candles who survived) and burn the
  people who took them.
- **Need:** to stop being only the thing that was done to her, and to trust
  people enough to let them help.
- **Flaw:** fire first. Suspicious, reckless and rude, and she pushes people
  away before they can hand her over.
- **Secret:** she knows two things nobody else in the party does:
  - she saw **Dace** at the mill that night, helping load children into
    Vosse's cart;
  - she saw **Wren Marr alive** after the Ashfields fire, being led away by
    keepers.

  She keeps both to herself because she thinks the lead, Dace's old friend,
  was in on it.
- **Pressure:** she's a heretic and a deserter. Every keeper and Hound would
  take her back, and Maud's report told them where she was.

## Arc

- **Start:** a hunted girl who trusts nobody and burns first.
- **End:** she swears her fire vow again, in front of the party, in her own
  words: "I burn so nobody else has to." In the finale she chooses to shield rather
  than burn. She finds the children, and some of them come home.

| Where | What changes |
| ----- | ------------ |
| Ch1 | Recognises the Veyne seal on Harl's orders; it was on the cart's passes. Tells the lead, coldly, that she's seen it before, and suspects the lead's family. |
| Ch3 | Tells the lead what Dace did at the mill, and hears Maud confess the report. Both relationships crack before they can heal. |
| Ch3 | (Also) the register shows she was a Candle, and Aske hears it. |
| Ch6 | At the Ashfields, tells Hollis that Wren was alive after the fire. Aske learns Rue stood on the fire line that burned her brother's company. |
| Ch7 | Side quest "The Other Cart" (a Candle who escaped with her). |
| Acts 2–3 | Swears her vow anew; finds the children. |

## Voice notes

- Short and rude. Answers questions with questions. Swears by the dead
  gods irreverently ("Vael's teeth"). Talks about food constantly; she was
  hungry for years. She is funny once she is safe and among people she has
  started to trust (downtime, supports); cornered, she is only sharp.
- Precise and cold when she talks about the Candles; no jokes there, ever.
- Calls the lead "heir" (sarcastic) until she trusts them. Calls Maud "Keeper"
  with a sneer, then "Maud" by the end.
- **Won't say:** "please". She'll say "give it here" instead.

**Sample lines**

- "No. I've hidden in enough cellars. I'm sworn to fire, Keeper, and you know
  it. I can burn them. I'm fighting."
- "I've seen that seal before, heir. On a cart. At night. With children in it."
- "Your friend lifted me in by the collar. He said 'up you go, little one.' He
  was very gentle about it."
