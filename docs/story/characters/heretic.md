# Rue

Id: `heretic` · [Name registry](../names.md)

## Role and class

- **Role:** playable companion from Chapter 1. She carries the truth about
  Dace and about Wren.
- **Class:** **Mage** (fire; swords in tiers 1–2).
- **Talent:** Mag.
- **Age:** 19.

## At a glance

A gifted girl from a Thornmarch charcoal-burners' family, taken twice:

- at 15 she was in Vosse's cart on the night at the mill, and escaped in the
  chaos after Vosse died;
- a year later she was caught by Vigil hunters, sworn to fire and made a
  **Candle**.

At 16 she burned at the Ashfields with the other Candles and deserted in the
smoke. There she swore a fire vow of her own, which makes her a heretic. Her
hands are scarred from that day. This autumn she took shelter with Keeper
Maud at Harrowby as her "cook" (she can't cook).

## Want, need, flaw, secret

- **Want:** to find the other children (the Candles who survived) and burn the
  people who took them.
- **Need:** to stop being only the thing that was done to her, and to trust
  people enough to let them help.
- **Flaw:** fire first. Suspicious, reckless and rude, and she pushes people
  away before they can hand her over.
- **Secret:** she knows two things nobody else in the party does:
  - she saw **Dace** at the mill that night, helping load children into
    Vosse's cart;
  - she saw **Wren Marr alive** after the Ashfields fire, being led away by
    keepers.

  She keeps both to herself because she thinks the lead, Dace's old friend,
  was in on it.
- **Pressure:** she's a heretic and a deserter. Every keeper and Hound would
  take her back, and Maud's report told them where she was.

## Arc

- **Start:** a hunted girl who trusts nobody and burns first.
- **End:** she swears her fire vow again, in front of the party, in her own
  words: "I burn so nobody else has to." In the finale she chooses to shield rather
  than burn. She finds the children, and some of them come home.

| Where | What changes |
| ----- | ------------ |
| Ch1 | Recognises the Veyne seal on Harl's orders; it was on the cart's passes. Tells the lead, coldly, that she's seen it before, and suspects the lead's family. |
| Ch3 | Tells the lead what Dace did at the mill, and hears Maud confess the report. Both relationships crack before they can heal. |
| Ch3 | (Also) the register shows she was a Candle, and Aske hears it. |
| Ch6 | At the Ashfields, tells Hollis that Wren was alive after the fire. Aske learns Rue stood on the fire line that burned her brother's company. |
| Ch7 | Side quest "The Other Cart" (a Candle who escaped with her). |
| Acts 2–3 | Swears her vow anew; finds the children. |

## Voice card

Written by 0737 (the `story-writing` skill, *Voices*). The sample lines
are illustrations of a situation, never lines for a script.

- **Age and experience:** nineteen; a charcoal-burners' kid, taken at
  fifteen, a Candle at sixteen, hunted for three years since. She knows
  fire, cellars, barracks talk, how to steal food and how to leave a town
  before dawn. She was hungry for years and food is always on her mind.
- **Temperature:** hot and sharp. About the Candles she goes cold and
  exact, and nothing else.
- **Openness:** says it angrily. Fear comes out as rudeness and as moving
  to the front.
- **When scared:** ruder; grabs things; goes forward. Cornered, she is
  only sharp; funny only once she is safe among people she has started to
  trust (downtime, supports).
- **Care signature:** food. She notices who hasn't eaten, because she
  didn't for years, and shoves bread at them without a word.
- **Fault the others put up with:** steals food, eats everyone's, can't
  cook, hogs the fire, glares, sleeps in her boots.
- **Line length and habit:** short. Answers a question with a question.
  Swears by the dead gods irreverently ("Vael's teeth"). Calls the lead
  "heir" (sarcastic) until she trusts them; Maud "Keeper" with a sneer,
  then "Maud" by the end.
- **Words from her life:** cellars, the barracks, bread, hands, fire,
  "the others" (the Candles who got out).
- **Won't say:** "please". She says "give it here".
- **Punctuation:** `?` more than anyone. A `!` when the fire won't come.
  Flat periods when it's the Candles.

**Sample lines** (one per situation; never copied into a script)

- *Under fire:* "Move. Move, I can't throw it past you-- is that your
  blood or his? Fine. Fine, then move."
- *At camp:* "Is anyone eating that? ...I'm asking because if nobody is
  eating that, then I am eating that."
- *Lying:* "I cook. I've always cooked. ...Why, what's wrong with it?"
- *Grieving:* "She cheated at knuckles. In the barracks. Every night, and
  nobody ever caught her at it, and I knew. ...Give me the bread, I'm not
  hungry, I just want to hold it."

Rewritten 2026-10-04 (ticket 0706) to fit the bought art, as Nick decided
in 0021 ("we can rewrite Rue's physical description as needed once we buy
the art"): Rue uses the Tiny Tales **Witch** hero (*Heroes: A New
Beginning*) for face, combat picture and map sprite. She was written with
chopped black hair and a stolen Ardevali greatcoat.

- **Silhouette:** small and sharp under a wide, battered blue witch's hat
  with a bent point, a gold band and a red feather.
- **Hair:** pale silver-blue, cut at the jaw, falling over one eye.
- **Face:** 19, thin, with green eyes that squint at everyone. The burn
  mark on her jaw is no longer part of her look; her burned hands are in
  the story but out of frame.
- **Clothing:** a dull red scarf wound high at the neck over a dark
  sleeveless top. She carries a staff and an orb in combat.
- **Colours:** deep blue, silver-blue, dull red, and ember orange for any
  fire.
- **Expressions** (the pack's name in brackets): `neutral`, `happy`
  (smile: a wink), `angry` (stern), `sad`, `surprised` (surprise), plus
  `sneer` (sly). Spare: `thinking`, `unique`.
