# Joss Pellam

Id: `defector` · [Name registry](../names.md)

## Role and class

- **Role:** playable companion from Chapter 2. The Hound who wouldn't, and
  the party's witness to the false flag.
- **Class:** **Swordsman** (outline). Level, stats and talent: 0848.
- **Age:** 18.
- **Joins:** in the scene after Kell's Ford (0069: a scripted join, no
  recruiting by talking).

## At a glance

A seamstress's son from a farm village in the valley heartland, the
eldest of four. He joined the Hounds a year ago for the pay and because
of Dace Marr: the servant's boy who became a war hero and a lord made a
farm boy think a nobody could rise. Dace knows him by name and once told
him his coat was a disgrace and fed him anyway. His lieutenant, Silas
Hendry, picked him for the company and called him "Needle", because he
can sew.

At Kell's Ford he refused to burn the houses. (How the player sees him
refuse is the Chapter 2 script's and battle's, 0744 and 0848.)

## Want, need, flaw, secret

- **Want:** to put Kell's Ford right: to say what happened to someone
  who can stop it, and be believed. Under that, to be able to go home and
  look his mother in the eye.
- **Need:** to stop looking for someone to follow (Silas, then Dace, now
  the lead) and decide what he thinks himself.
- **Flaw:** hero-worship, and wanting to be liked. He tells people what
  they want to hear, and believes the best of anyone who is kind to him,
  Dace most of all.
- **Secret:** he sewed the Brennish colors. Silas set him to it in the
  week before Kell's Ford ("for a ruse against the hill bands"), and he
  stitched forty badges by lamplight and was proud of them. He knew what
  they were for by the second night, and finished them. He says he only
  carried them.
- **Pressure:** the Hounds hang deserters, and his face is known in
  every Hound post in the heartland. His mother and three younger sisters
  live in Hound country (Chapter 4's roads).

## Arc

- **Start:** a frightened boy who did one brave thing and wants someone
  to tell him it was right.
- **End of Act 1:** a deserter with nothing to go home to who has said
  the worst thing he did out loud, in front of everyone, and is still
  here.
- **Acts 2–3:** stops following and starts choosing. When Dace confesses
  in Act 3, Joss is one of the people who knew him as a captain (the act
  tickets decide what he does with that).

## Voice card

Written by 0743 (the `story-writing` skill, *Voices*). The sample lines
are illustrations of a situation, never lines for a script.

- **Age and experience:** eighteen; a farm boy, a year in the Hounds, and
  before that his mother's workroom. He knows cloth, stitches, drill, the
  Hound rule book, cows, and his sisters' business. He runs on, asks too
  much, overstates, and is embarrassed when he says too much. He wants
  very badly to be taken seriously.
- **Temperature:** warm and eager, with nerves underneath.
- **Openness:** says it straight and too much, then tries to take half
  of it back. The one thing he hides (the badges) he hides badly.
- **When scared:** faster and more, then the drill: Hound words, "yes,
  Lieutenant", counting the enemy aloud. The drill is what holds him up.
- **Care signature:** clothes. A torn cuff, a missing button, a coat too
  thin for the weather. He mends without asking, and talks while he does
  it.
- **Fault the others put up with:** talks about his sisters (the eldest
  and the twins) whether or not anyone asked; mends things nobody wanted mended;
  calls the lead "Your Grace" long after he's told not to (Tamsin can't
  get over it; the title has been gone four years); needs to be told he
  did well.
- **Line length and habit:** long and running, then a short question
  asking whether that was right ("That was right, wasn't it?"). Corrects
  himself mid-line.
- **Words from his life:** his ma, the twins, his sisters, the farm, the company,
  "the lieutenant", "the captain", thread, a needle, the workroom.
- **Won't say:** anything against Dace, until Chapter 4. "The captain
  didn't know." "The captain wouldn't."
- **Punctuation:** `?` most (approval, mostly), `--` when he corrects
  himself, a `!` of delight in downtime about one box in six, none in a
  fight unless he's calling a name.

**Sample lines** (one per situation; never copied into a script)

- *Under fire:* "Roof. Three on the roof-- three, there's one behind the
  chimney. They'll come down by the well. Do I take the well? I'm taking
  the well."
- *At camp:* "Hold still, it's only the cuff. My ma does a stitch you
  can't even see. I'm not as good as her, but-- there. You can't see it.
  Can you? You can a bit."
- *Lying:* "The badges came from Varenhall, all done. I only carried the
  bundle. ...They were backstitched, though. Who backstitches a badge?
  Nobody does that."
- *Grieving:* "He called me Needle. Nobody else ever did. ...I'm allowed
  to not like that he's dead, aren't I? I can just not like it?"

## Planted for after

The skill's *Grief and death*: said once, early, in a quiet scene.

- **Plan for after:** to make a coat. One whole coat, his own, from cloth
  he picked, start to finish. He has only ever sewn other people's
  repairs and forty badges.
- **In his hands:** a flat tin of needles and a card of black thread. It
  comes out whenever anyone sits still near him.
