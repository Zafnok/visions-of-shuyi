# Ragna Holt

Id: `envoy` · [Name registry](../names.md)

## Role and class

- **Role:** the Brennish envoy in Saltmere (an NPC, not playable). The
  face of Brennmark's peace party in Act 1; she brings Brennmark to the
  lead's side in Act 3 (outline).
- **Class:** none. Whether she is a green unit to protect in the Envoy's
  House is 0851's.
- **Age:** 38.

## At a glance

A Brennish hall-holder's daughter who became a negotiator in the war,
and wrote most of the Ash Peace three years ago. Her name is on it.
Brennmark's hardliners call her the woman who signed away the Ashfields'
dead. Now the "Brennish raids" are breaking her treaty one village at a
time, and she has been in Saltmere for most of a year buying news and
writing letters, trying to prove the raids are staged. She has a son of
eight at home she hasn't seen since spring. Hedda, her foster-sister, is
the one person she doesn't have to be careful with.

## Want, need, flaw, secret

- **Want:** to keep the peace she signed: proof that the raids are
  staged, put in front of the people who decide.
- **Need:** to learn that proof doesn't stop a war; people do. She has to
  trust people, not paper.
- **Flaw:** she trusts procedure and evidence over people, and uses
  people as evidence. She makes Joss tell it again until he breaks, and
  thinks that's kindness, because it makes the testimony hold.
- **Secret:** she has been buying Ardevali march news from the same
  Brennish smuggler Aske sold to. That's how she knows Aske's name
  (outline, Chapter 5).
- **Pressure:** the League is neutral and for sale; a magistrate sells
  her safe house to the Hounds. The hardliners at home are waiting for her
  to fail.

## Arc

- **Start:** a tired negotiator who believes the right proof will stop
  the war.
- **Act 1:** she gets the proof (Joss's testimony) and is expelled for
  it. She sends it to the Ashfields with the party and Hedda. The
  commander believes it and the war comes anyway (Chapter 6): her flaw's
  answer, offstage. In Chapter 8 her ship takes the party east.
- **Act 3:** she brings Brennmark, with Aske, as people, not as a treaty
  (the act tickets decide how).

## Voice card

Written by 0743 (the `story-writing` skill, *Voices*). The sample lines
are illustrations of a situation, never lines for a script.

- **Age and experience:** thirty-eight; a hall-holder's daughter, a war
  negotiator, a year in a foreign port. She knows treaties, ships, prices,
  who owes whom, how to make a room wait. She has had no sleep worth the
  name in a month.
- **Temperature:** cool and brisk. Warm only with Hedda, and about her
  son.
- **Openness:** says what she needs, plainly; hides what she feels behind
  what she needs.
- **When scared:** crisper. Gives orders in short sentences and counts
  things.
- **Care signature:** whether people have been paid and fed. She asks
  what something cost them, and pays it.
- **Fault the others put up with:** makes people repeat themselves
  ("Again."); impatient with ceremony; hates the southern heat and says
  so; treats a conversation like a deposition.
- **Line length and habit:** short, conditional ("If you can prove that,
  then..."). Counts days. Says "Again." when she wants it told once more.
- **Words from her life:** the treaty, the hall, her son, the fjord,
  ships, "the League", days.
- **Won't say:** "war". She says "this trouble", until Chapter 5's turn,
  when she finally does.
- **Punctuation:** flat periods and direct questions. A `...` only about
  her son; a `!` almost never.

**Sample lines** (one per situation; never copied into a script)

- *Under fire:* "Shutters. All of them. Hedda, the boy stays where I can
  see him."
- *Off duty (with Hedda):* "Nine days, and the League wants it in
  writing. Three copies. ...Is there tea? Real tea, not the hot water
  they sell here."
- *Lying:* "Your archer? I've never heard her name. Why would I have
  heard her name?"
- *Grieving:* "He'll be taller. They tell you that, in letters. Taller.
  ...Read it to me again. From the part about the boots."
