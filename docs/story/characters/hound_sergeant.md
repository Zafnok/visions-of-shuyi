# Sergeant Ned Tolley

Id: `hound_sergeant` · [Name registry](../names.md)

A short boss sheet (0743): want, voice, three lines.

## Role

- **The Chapter 3 boss** at Greywater Abbey: the Hound sergeant who holds
  the abbey for the prior while the records burn, at the scriptorium
  (the Seize point). He dies there.
- **Why him, not the prior:** the ticket offered either. The prior
  ([`greywater_prior`](greywater_prior.md)) is the person Maud has to
  face, and she breaks with the Vigil by taking the register, not by
  killing her own prior; so the prior lives, and the man with the sword
  is the boss.
- **Class, level and stats:** 0849.
- **Age:** 47.

## Who he is

A guardsman of House Veyne for fifteen years under Hollis. He stood the
gate at Veyne Hall when the lead and Dace were boys, and taught Dace to
hold a sword before Hollis did. When the house lost its title and the
lead went into exile, he stayed with the hall; when the hall went to
Dace, Dace kept the old guard on and made them Hounds. Ned has been
sent to Greywater because it is a quiet post for an old man.

- **Want:** to keep his post and his pension, and to serve the house
  whoever holds it. He wants this to be a quiet night.
- **His grievance, which a player could share:** "The house fed me
  twenty years. When the title went, who fed us? The boy did."
- **With Hollis** (who shares his history, so a 2–4 box exchange): his
  old captain, who went into exile and left the rest of them behind.

## Voice

- A tired old guardsman: short, practical, a little sad, honest. He
  calls Hollis "Captain" and Dace "the boy", as Hollis does.
- **Won't say:** that the boy is wrong.
- **Punctuation:** flat periods; a `...` when he looks at Hollis.

**Sample lines** (never copied into a script)

- *Engage (anyone):* "Go back over the causeway and I never saw you. I
  mean that. I'm too old for this, and so's the prior."
- *With Hollis:* "Captain. ...You got old. Fifteen years I stood your
  gate. Don't ask me to stand aside from this one."
- *Defeat:* "Your bay mare. At the hall. Nobody rides her, Captain, they
  never-- she's still yours."

## Planted

- **In his hands:** an old Veyne-blue scarf, worn under the Hound black.
