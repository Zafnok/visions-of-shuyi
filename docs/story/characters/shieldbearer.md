# Hedda Ravn

Id: `shieldbearer` · [Name registry](../names.md)

## Role and class

- **Role:** playable companion from Chapter 5. Ragna's bodyguard, "lent"
  to the lead to make sure they get to the Ashfields. The second
  Brennish voice in the party, and the oldest after Hollis.
- **Class:** **Raider** (outline; axes). Level, stats and talent: 0851.
- **Age:** 44.
- **Joins:** in Chapter 5, loaned by Ragna "to make sure you get there".

## At a glance

A fisherman's daughter from a Brennish fjord. Her mother nursed Ragna
Holt, and Hedda grew up in the Holts' hall as Ragna's foster-sister, six
years older and twice as big. She fought the whole Border War in a
Brennish shield company, including the early years of raiding across the
Thornmarch fords, and has been Ragna's bodyguard since the Ash Peace.
She has never been more than a room away from Ragna in three years, and
Ragna has just sent her across a country at war with strangers.

## Want, need, flaw, secret

- **Want:** to get the proof to the Brennish commander, and then get back
  to Ragna before anything happens to her.
- **Need:** to choose a side herself, and not only because the house she
  was raised in pointed her at it.
- **Flaw:** loyalty that doesn't ask questions. She will not hear a word
  against Ragna, and she doesn't trust southerners until each one has
  earned it separately.
- **Pressure:** Ragna is alone in a city that just sold her safe house to
  the Hounds, and every day Hedda is gone is a day nobody is at her door.
- **Not a secret:** she raided the Thornmarch in the war, burned barns
  and drove off cattle, and she says so plainly when Kell's Ford comes up.
  She isn't proud of it and isn't hiding it. (It is what Joss's badges
  were made to look like.)

## Arc

- **Start:** a loaned guard doing a job: "I'm not yours, outlaw. I'm
  lent."
- **End of Act 1:** she gets on the ship east because she has decided to,
  not because she was lent.
- **Acts 2–3:** in Act 3 she stands with Ragna and Aske when Brennmark
  comes in (the act tickets decide how).

## Voice card

Written by 0743 (the `story-writing` skill, *Voices*). The sample lines
are illustrations of a situation, never lines for a script.

- **Age and experience:** forty-four; a fishing boat, a hall, twenty
  years of war and guard duty. She knows boats, weather, ice, axes,
  shields, how to stand at a door all night, and how to make a big room
  calm. Unhurried. She has seen everything once and is surprised by
  southerners anyway.
- **Temperature:** warm and slow.
- **Openness:** says it straight and kindly, and doesn't elaborate. On
  Ragna, says nothing at all.
- **When scared:** slower and calmer; lower voice; puts a hand on
  someone. "Easy."
- **Care signature:** cold. Who is shivering, whose hands are blue, who
  is sleeping without a blanket. She gives her own cloak and says she
  never feels the cold, which is true.
- **Fault the others put up with:** mothers everyone, Aske most, which
  Aske can't stand; snores like a saw; eats salt herring that clears a
  room; laughs from the belly at small things at the wrong time.
- **Line length and habit:** short, warm statements. Few questions. She
  calls the lead "outlaw" (fondly, after Chapter 5) and Aske "hill girl";
  everyone younger than her is "little one" when they are hurt.
- **Words from her life:** the boat, the fjord, her mother, Ragna, "the
  hall", herring, ice, the shield wall.
- **Won't say:** that Ragna is wrong, about anything.
- **Punctuation:** warm periods. A `!` when she laughs (downtime) or calls
  someone across a field. She almost never trails off; when she does, it
  is about home.

**Sample lines** (one per situation; never copied into a script)

- *Under fire:* "Easy. Easy, little one. Arrow first, then breathe.
  There. Again."
- *At camp:* "Come here. Your hands are blue. Give them. I'm always warm,
  it's the herring. ...Southerners. You don't know how to be cold."
- *Lying:* "Ragna? She sleeps fine. She's always slept fine." (Ragna
  hasn't slept a night through in a month.)
- *Grieving:* "He ate like a hall dog. My mother would have loved him.
  ...Pass me the knife. I'll finish the boat."

## Planted for after

- **Plan for after:** to take Ragna's son out on the fjord for his first
  cod. He is eight and has never been in a boat. She has promised him the
  big one.
- **In her hands:** a small knife and a half-carved wooden boat for the
  boy, worked on at every fire. It gets a mast in Chapter 6.
