## Role

- **An NPC in Chapter 3:** the prior of Greywater Abbey, the Vigil's
  house for the Thornmarch, burning the register's records under Hound
  guard (outline). He doesn't fight, and he lives; what he does when the
  party takes the scriptorium is the script's (0745).
- **Age:** 63.

## Who he is

Prior of Greywater for twenty years. Every keeper in the Thornmarch
writes to him once a quarter, Maud included, and he copies what matters
into the register and sends it on to Ossary. He sent Maud's report about
a gifted girl with burned hands up to Ossary in his own hand. He wrote
the Candles' names into the register himself, in his best hand, because
he thought the children deserved it. Now Ossary has ordered the records
burned, and he is doing it.

- **Want:** to keep his abbey and his keepers safe, and to keep the
  Vigil's name clean.
- **What he tells himself, which a player could share:** the register in
  outlaw hands is a hunting list. "Every name in that book has a mother."
- **With Maud:** her prior for six years. Kind to her, proud of her,
  and the man her report went through.

## Voice

- A frightened, kindly old schoolmaster of a priest, not a schemer: he
  calls Maud "child", fusses, and talks too fast when he's caught.
- **Won't say:** Crane's name. He says "Ossary".
- **Punctuation:** questions and `--`; a `!` at the fire.

**Sample lines** (never copied into a script)

- *Caught (to Maud):* "Child, go home. Go home and write that you found
  the abbey empty, and I'll write the same, and by morning we'll both be
  telling the truth."
- *Lying:* "There's no register of that kind. There never was. These are
  tithe rolls."
- *Grieving:* "I wrote every one of them in my best hand. I thought the
  children deserved my best hand."
