# Prior Edwin Sallow

Id: `greywater_prior` · [Name registry](../names.md)

A short boss sheet (0743): want, voice, three lines.

## Role

- **The Chapter 3 boss** at Greywater Abbey: the prior burning the
  register's records under Hound guard (outline), holding the
  scriptorium (the Seize point). Nick made him the boss, not the Hound
  sergeant guarding him (0070; Nick, 2026-10-28, 0743).
- **When beaten** he dies or surrenders (Nick, 2026-10-28: "can say dies
  or surrenders"); the Chapter 3 script and battle (0745, 0849) pick
  one. Either way Maud takes the register from him: if he lives, she
  takes it from his hands; if he dies, from his desk.
- **Class, level and stats:** 0849.
- **Age:** 63.
- **Guarded by:** Sergeant Ned Tolley ([`hound_sergeant`](hound_sergeant.md)),
  a named Hound on the walls.

## Who he is

Prior of Greywater for twenty years. Every keeper in the Thornmarch
writes to him once a quarter, Maud included, and he copies what matters
into the register and sends it on to Ossary. He sent Maud's report about
a gifted girl with burned hands up to Ossary in his own hand. He wrote
the Candles' names into the register himself, in his best hand, because
he thought the children deserved it. Now Ossary has ordered the records
burned, and he is doing it, and he will fight to finish.

- **Want:** to keep his abbey and his keepers safe, and to keep the
  Vigil's name clean.
- **His grievance, which a player could share:** the register in outlaw
  hands is a hunting list. "Every name in that book has a mother."
- **With Maud** (who shares his history, so a 2–4 box exchange): her
  prior for six years. Kind to her, proud of her, and the man her report
  went through.

## Voice

- A frightened, kindly old schoolmaster of a priest, not a schemer: he
  calls Maud "child", fusses, and talks too fast when he's caught. He
  fights the way a frightened man does, without stopping talking.
- **Won't say:** Crane's name. He says "Ossary".
- **Punctuation:** questions and `--`; a `!` at the fire.

**Sample lines** (never copied into a script)

- *Engage (anyone):* "Stay back from the fire! There's nothing here for
  you. There's nothing here for anyone, by morning."
- *With Maud:* "Child, go home. Go home and write that you found the
  abbey empty, and I'll write the same, and by morning we'll both be
  telling the truth."
- *Defeat (dies):* "The ink. The blue ink is for the novices, don't let
  them waste it on-- on copying..."
- *Defeat (surrenders):* "Take it. Take it, then. I wrote every one of
  them in my best hand. I thought the children deserved my best hand."

## Planted

- **In his hands:** the register's red ledgers, an armful, on their way to
  the fire.
