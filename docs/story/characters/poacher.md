# Aske

Id: `poacher` · [Name registry](../names.md)

## Role and class

- **Role:** playable companion from Chapter 1. Brennmark's voice in the party.
- **Class:** **Archer**.
- **Talent:** Dex.
- **Age:** 17.

## At a glance

A Brennish girl from a hill-hold near the Ashfields. Her older brother Tor was
one of the soldiers who burned there. Aske came south with the refugees after
the Ash Peace, lives poaching in the Thornmarch woods, and sends every coin
home to her mother. She hates Ardevali nobles on principle, and the lead is one.

## Want, need, flaw, secret

- **Want:** to go home with enough silver that her mother never has to beg,
  and to see someone pay for the Ashfields.
- **Need:** to see people instead of flags, and to decide who she fights for,
  not where she was born.
- **Flaw:** quick, total hatred, and pride that won't let her take back a
  word.
- **Secret:** she's been selling news of the march (patrols, fords, who's
  armed) to Brennish raiders across the border. It started as survival.
- **Pressure:** in Chapter 2 the "Brennish raiders" who burn Kell's Ford turn
  out to be Ardevali Hounds in Brennish colours, which is exactly the kind of
  thing her news could have helped someone arrange.

## Arc

- **Start:** a refugee who thinks every Ardevali is guilty of the Ashfields.
- **End:** the bridge between the two kingdoms. In Act 3 she stands with the
  Brennish envoy and the lead, and says aloud what the Brennish lost and what
  Ardeval did, both. She goes home, and her mother can finally stop begging.

| Where | What changes |
| ----- | ------------ |
| Ch1 | Scouts for the lead because Harl's men killed deer in her woods and left them to rot. That's the reason she gives; the truth is she likes Harrowby. |
| Ch2 | Sees through the false flag at Kell's Ford. Her hatred loses its target. |
| Ch5 | Her spying comes out in Saltmere: her buyer, a Brennish smuggler, also sells to the envoy's people, and Ragna knows her name. The lead's reaction (a reply tone) matters to her. Side quest "Letters Home". |
| Ch3 | The Greywater register says Rue was a Candle. Aske stops speaking to her. |
| Ch6 | At the Ashfields she learns Rue stood on the fire line that burned her brother's company. The hardest scene of her arc. |
| Act 3 | Brings the envoy and the lead together; speaks for Brennmark. |

## Voice card

Written by 0737 (the `story-writing` skill, *Voices*). The sample lines
are illustrations of a situation, never lines for a script.

- **Age and experience:** seventeen; a hill-hold girl who has hunted since
  she was twelve and lived alone in the woods for three years. She means
  every word, cannot take one back, and asks what a word means when she
  doesn't know it. She reads badly (Maud is teaching her). Skills the life
  didn't teach don't appear: no court manners, no history, no irony.
- **Temperature:** cold surface over a hot center (hatred, pride).
- **Openness:** says the true thing bluntly, sometimes the cruel one, and
  then stands by it with her jaw out. She will say she is angry; never
  that she is afraid or cold.
- **When scared:** still, literal, angry. She counts things, and it comes
  out as a hunter's judgment of what people hit and what they waste.
- **Care signature:** food, and whether people are wet. She leaves rabbits
  on doorsteps, undercharges people who can't pay, and would deny both.
- **Fault the others put up with:** pride; sulks; smells of smoke; never
  says thank you; is seventeen and sure.
- **Line length and habit:** very short, flat, literal. No contractions
  ("I cannot", "do not"). Ardevali idioms trip her up. Calls the lead
  "noble" until she doesn't; the day she says "Veyne" is a quiet beat.
- **Words from her life:** *mor*, *hus*, "my brother", the hill, the
  woods, "my deer", arrows (she counts them).
- **Won't say:** "Ardeval" without a sneer, until Act 3.
- **Punctuation:** periods and questions. A `!` about once a chapter, and
  it is anger.

**Sample lines** (one per situation; never copied into a script)

- *Under fire:* "Two more. Behind the cart. ...I said behind the cart,
  why are you looking at the barn."
- *At camp:* "You cooked this? ...It is not bad. It is not good. Give me
  the pot, you are burning the bottom."
- *Lying:* "I was at the ford. All night. Setting lines. ...Fish lines."
  (She can't do it either: she adds detail, and the detail is wrong.)
- *Grieving:* "He could not whistle. My brother. He tried for years. ...I
  am going to check the snares."

- **Clothing:** a short brown cloak over a green tunic with pale trim, grey
  leggings and laced boots.
- **Colours:** straw blond, moss green, brown and violet.
