# Warden Constance Hobb

Id: `undercroft_warden` · [Name registry](../names.md)

A short boss sheet (0743): want, voice, three lines.

## Role

- **The Chapter 7 boss** in the Undercroft: **Crane's champion**
  (outline). Crane leaves; she stays to hold the stairs. She dies there.
- **Class, level and stats:** 0853.
- **Age:** 52.

## Who she is

For six years the Vigil warden who kept the Candles' barracks under
Ossary. She woke them, fed them, dressed their burns, sat with the ones
who cried, and locked the door at night. She walked them to the carts
for the Ashfields. Crane told her death is only a door, and that she
will see the ones who burned again. She believes him, because she can't
bear not to. Now the dormitory has been emptied (where the children went is the
Chapter 7 script's, 0749); Crane told her they were "going home", and
she chose to believe that too. She still airs the blankets.

- **Want:** her children back: to see the burned ones again, as Crane
  promised, and the others under her roof.
- **Her grievance, which a player could share:** she loved them, and she
  was told she would get them back.
- **With Rue** (who shares her history, so a 2–4 box exchange): Rue was
  one of hers. Rue gave her bread to the little ones.

## Voice

- A big, gentle, tired nurse's voice: "love", "now then", and questions
  about whether you've eaten. She is not cruel to anyone, and that is
  what makes her hard to fight. Played sad, never creepy, in the spirit of
  the outline's rules for the prince: no horror staging.
- **Won't say:** that they're dead. "Gone ahead." "Away."
- **Punctuation:** soft questions and `...`; no `!`.

**Sample lines** (never copied into a script)

- *Engage (anyone):* "Go back up the stairs. There's nobody down here
  now. ...There's nobody down here."
- *With Rue:* "Rue? Let me see your hands. ...Oh, love. Who did your
  hands?"
- *Defeat:* "The little ones' blankets are in the press. Somebody air
  them... they'll want them aired."

## Planted

- **In her hands:** the barracks keys on a ring at her belt, one for
  every door.
