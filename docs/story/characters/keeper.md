# Keeper Maud

Id: `keeper` · [Name registry](../names.md)

## Role and class

- **Role:** playable companion from Chapter 1. The party's healer, and the
  Vigil seen from inside.
- **Class:** **Cleric** (healing vows; gauntlets).
- **Talent:** Res.
- **Age:** 27.

## At a glance

The Vigil's keeper of Harrowby for six years: anxious, fussy, genuinely kind,
and physically unable to lie. She believes in the vows and in the Vigil,
because the Vigil taught her to heal. She tested Wren Marr and sent her name to
the Vigil three years ago. This autumn she sheltered Rue, a gifted girl with
burned hands, and **dutifully reported her**, asking the Vigil to help her
gently. That report is why the killers came.

## Want, need, flaw, secret

- **Want:** to be a good keeper: heal the sick, keep her vows, and keep the
  Vigil's peace.
- **Need:** to learn that faith isn't obedience. She can keep her vows to the
  dead gods while breaking with the living Vigil.
- **Flaw:** obedience, and conflict avoidance. She assumes the institution
  means well because *she* does.
- **Secret:** she wrote both reports, Wren's three years ago and Rue's this
  autumn. She learns what the second one caused in Chapter 1, and confesses
  it to Rue in Chapter 3.
- **Pressure:** leaving Harrowby with the lead makes her an apostate as well as
  an outlaw.

## Arc

- **Start:** an obedient keeper who trusts the Vigil completely.
- **End:** a keeper without a Vigil. In Act 3 she stands against Crane, her
  superior, in front of other keepers, and some follow her. Faith kept,
  institution refused.

| Where | What changes |
| ----- | ------------ |
| Ch1 | Learns Harl came for "the girl with burned hands", and realises her own letter sent them. |
| Ch3 | Takes the Greywater register; confesses to Rue. Breaks with the Vigil. |
| Ch7 | Faces Crane in Ossary; side quest "Unwritten": she returns the names in the register to the families. |
| Act 3 | Leads the keepers who refuse the Door. |

## Voice notes

- Long, polite sentences that interrupt themselves; qualifiers ("I think,
  possibly, with respect..."). In quiet scenes her fussing is funny. Under
  real danger it isn't played for laughs (`setting-and-tone.md`, *Where the
  jokes go*): she gets shorter, apologizes and says the plain thing.
- Quotes vows and small prayers to Ama, then adds a practical comment ("Ama
  keep you. Also, keep pressure on that."): a downtime habit.
- Can't lie. When she tries, she fails out loud ("I was not... that is, I have
  no... yes. Yes, I did.").
- **Won't say:** anything cruel, even to Crane. Her defiance is polite, which
  makes it land harder.

**Sample lines**

- (Downtime, a scraped knee) "Ama keep you. Also, and I say this as a
  keeper, please keep pressure on that."
- (Under threat) "Please go down. It is you they have come for."
- "I wrote to them. About you. I asked them to be gentle. I thought... I
  thought that was a thing you could ask."
- "With respect, Master Crane, and I do mean that, I have a great deal of
  respect: no."

## Portrait brief

Rewritten 2026-10-04 (ticket 0040) to fit the bought art: the keeper uses
the Tiny Tales **Church Cleric** (*Battlers 5: Faith and Evil*) for her
combat picture and map sprite. She was written as a man of 34 called Piers;
Nick chose the art and asked for a name that doesn't read as a man's.

- **Silhouette:** small and slight under the Vigil keeper's brown hood and
  short cape, with a ring-headed staff.
- **Hair:** teal green, cut at the jaw, showing under the hood.
- **Face:** late twenties, round and kind, with worried eyebrows and ink
  stains on her fingers when her hand is in frame. Light-to-medium shading.
- **Clothing:** a brown hood and cape over a pale short robe, and boots.
  The art has no stole or lantern charm; the story no longer mentions them.
- **Colours:** hood brown, teal green, cream and candle yellow.
- **Expressions:** `neutral`, `happy`, `angry` (rare: a frown more than a
  scowl), `sad`, `surprised`, plus `flustered` (wide eyes, mouth open
  mid-word).
- **Dialogue face** (ticket 0706; Nick picked it from three and made it in
  the Character Generator, 2026-10-11): green side-swept hair to the jaw,
  green eyes, and a cream hooded top with the hood down over a grey collar
  with a clasp. The generator has no raised hood, so in dialogue her hood
  is down and cream, not brown as in her combat picture. Expressions (the
  generator's name in brackets): `neutral`, `happy` (smile), `angry`
  (stern), `sad`, `surprised` (surprise), `flustered` (surprise). Spare:
  `happy`, `smug`, `thinking`.
  Her eyes were softened in ticket 0730 (Nick found the generator's
  "too scary... cat-like"): wider irises and a softer lid, by Claude's
  small edit (`docs/design/look-and-feel.md`).
