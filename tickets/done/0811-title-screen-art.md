# 0811 — Title screen art

## Context

The title screen (`crates/ui/src/screens/title.rs`) is plain text: the words
`tactical-rpg` and "an ASCII tactics game" above the menu. It is the first
thing every player sees, and store pages and itch.io screenshots (09xx) will
show it. 0012 decides the game's name; 0801 builds the real title menu. No
ticket covered the title's look. Found while listing Chapter 1 gaps
(2026-09-29).

Look and feel rules: `docs/design/look-and-feel.md` (0011), ADR-0018 (palette
names only, glyph and half-block techniques). Nick disliked Claude-drawn
*portraits* (0021), so offer lettering and scene options, not only drawn
figures, and expect several rounds.

**Added 2026-10-01:** Nick also wants an intro cinematic on the title
(ticket 0036, built by 0817–0820). It is as long as the title song and ends
on "our logo whatever it might be", held during the song's quiet pause
(about 17 seconds). So the art chosen here is also the cinematic's last
shot: it must look right alone on the screen, with no menu under it. 0036
did not wait for this ticket (it used a stand-in logo). It is decided
(2026-10-03, *Intro cinematic* in `docs/design/title-screen.md`): the logo
and the menu show together after the first press and during the song's
silence; after 15 idle seconds the menu fades and the cinematic takes the
screen; a press brings logo and menu back. So the logo is always seen
**with** the menu under it, and the cinematic covers the whole screen in
between. When
offering option C below, tell Nick the cinematic already covers moving
pictures on the title.

## Completion notes

**Done 2026-10-12.** Nick chose over four rounds of renders; his words and
the rules are in `docs/design/look-and-feel.md`, *Title screen*.

- **The look:** block lettering (`VISIONS`, a small lowercase `of` between
  two gold bars, `SHUYI` big and gold; Nick: "I like pale of, gold bars") over a bought battle backdrop that
  changes each launch (seven pictures), with sparks drifting up. No line
  under the title. The music starts at once, as before.
- **How it is built** (ADR-0059): the logo is a PNG generated by `cargo
  xtask title-art` from `assets-src/title/logo.txt` and the palette, drawn
  as one sprite; `assets/title/title.ron` names it and the backdrops
  (`trpg_content::title`, checked with the other assets); the backdrops
  here are flat placeholders, the bought ones are in the private
  repository (`cargo xtask title-art --bought`). `title::glyph::paint_logo`
  paints the logo shot alone, for 0817.
- **Screenshot:** `docs/screenshots/0811-title.png` (the game with the
  bought art).

**Deviations from the ticket:**

- The art is **not glyph cells in a text file under `assets/`**. Nick
  picked lettering over a picture, and ADR-0038 (newer than this ticket)
  says a picture is a sprite from an image file. The text file is the
  logo's source (`assets-src/title/logo.txt`); the game loads the PNG made
  from it.
- `TITLE`/`SUBTITLE` were already text keys; `title.title` and
  `title.subtitle` are removed (the name is a picture now, and there is no
  second line). The test language's stale entry moved to `title.continue`.
- The window title and the web page title already said *Visions of Shuyi*
  (0905): nothing to do.
- New: `Ctx::idle_motion` (off in tests, on in `app` and `frame-png`),
  so the sparks don't make every test of the title depend on the time.
- Downstream tickets 0817, 0819 and 0830 got a note on how to draw the
  logo.

**Rules I had to decide (*Claude's starting rules*; Nick can veto):**

- One backdrop per launch, random, never the same twice in a row, kept for
  the whole launch. (Nick: "yes sounds good".)
- The sparks show from the first frame, before the first press too, and
  pass behind the logo and the menu.
- `Press any key or button` and a notice under the menu sit on a dark band
  so they read on a bright backdrop.

**For Nick when playing:** each start shows another backdrop; say which
ones don't work under the lettering (the bright day one is in the pool),
and whether the sparks want to be fewer, slower or bigger.
