# 0075 — Decide: the Moonblade's long optional questline

## Context

Ticket 0018 decided the Moonblade (`docs/design/combat-arts.md`, *Special
weapons*): Nick's Moonlight Greatsword, whose strikes hit Res and whose
art throws a wave. Nick: "I think MLGS should be the end of a very long
optional questline like they did in Elden Ring". Its stats are
placeholders until the questline is placed.

## Nick input

**Decision**, with the `ask-nick` skill, when Act 2's outline is being
written (the questline likely spans acts).

## Questions to ask

1. **Where it starts and ends:** which act opens it, how many steps, and
   whether it crosses acts (Elden Ring's Ranni questline; FE's
   paralogues; Tactics Ogre's optional chains).
2. **Whose story it is:** a recruit's, the world's, or the weapon's own
   (the blade's past).
3. **Can it be missed for good,** like side quests (`world-structure.md`,
   with a reminder), or always open?
4. **The Moonblade's final stats and rank**, once its act is known.

## What to record

`world-structure.md` (the questline), `docs/story/outline.md` (its
steps), `combat-arts.md` (the Moonblade's stats); the content tickets
that build it.
